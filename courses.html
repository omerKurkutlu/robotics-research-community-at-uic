Robotics Courses at UIC

Robotics-related courses offered across UIC's engineering departments, compiled from the UIC Academic Catalog and the ECE Robotics Laboratory resource list. Core robotics courses cover robots directly (kinematics, manipulators, mechatronics, HRI, wearable robots); Related courses cover the supporting areas — control, computer vision, machine learning, estimation and dynamics. Course numbers and availability change each year; confirm in the catalog and the term schedule.
